Fluent Scrollbar & Scroll View

Native scrolling with geometry-driven overlay scrollbars, additive smooth glide, merged heading markers, and Top-Layer hover cards (also powering this page's <k-scrollbar>).

1. Native Scrolling, Overlay Bars

<k-scroll-view> keeps native browser wheel, touchpad inertia, keyboard navigation, find-in-page, and scrollIntoView() intact while drawing crisp overlay bars directly over the content box.

Because every bar and hover card carries pointer-events: none, hidden scrollbars never block hover states or clicks on underlying elements. Input is routed strictly by pointer geometry within each bar and its hot zone.

2. Additive Glide & Exponential Chase

Clicks on arrow buttons, track paging, track jumps, and markers drive a frame-rate-independent exponential glide (τ = 50ms). Rapid steps add directly onto the active goal rather than restarting, and any external wheel or touch scroll yields the glide immediately.

Meanwhile, the drawn thumb and markers smoothly chase layout changes (τ = 100ms) while collapsing to instant updates during direct thumb drags or under prefers-reduced-motion.

3. Section Markers & Top-Layer Cards

Pass marker-selector="h4" or assign the markers property to plot jump ticks along the track. Nearby ticks within --k-scrollbar-marker-spacing automatically merge into a single mark whose Top-Layer Popover card lists every grouped destination.

4. Accessibility & System Preferences

An internal MutationObserver adds tabindex="0" to the viewport only when no focusable descendant exists (WCAG 2.1.1), keeping text-only regions keyboard scrollable without inserting redundant Tab stops.

Under forced-colors: active or @media print, the custom overlay hides automatically and native system scrollbars take over cleanly.

5. Fluent, Classic & Minimal Variants

Switch between Fluent (WinUI expanding pill + frosted track + caret arrows), Classic (Windows 8/10 17px square bar + 33px chevron buttons), and Minimal (slim floating pill) — all themed automatically via @kerno/tokens.
